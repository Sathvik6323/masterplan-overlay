Disclaimer

This tool is for educational purposes only.

When placing points, please be as accurate as possible — the quality of the georeferenced output depends entirely on the precision of your control points. Use recognizable landmarks like road intersections, building corners, or other permanent features.

Master plan data sourced from the Directorate of Town & Country Planning, Govt. of Andhra Pradesh. This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body.

How to Contribute

  1. Select a city from the dropdown — the official PDF link will appear.
  2. Download the file and upload it here (or upload your own file).
  3. Click on the master plan (left) at a recognizable landmark (road intersection, building corner).
  4. Click the same spot on the satellite map (right). A numbered pair is created.
  5. Repeat 10–12 times with points spread across the city. More = better accuracy.
  6. Click Download GCP to get the QGIS-compatible file.
  7. Click Submit via Issue to open a GitHub Issue with your file attached.

Tip: Pick points far apart from each other (different corners of the city) for best results.
