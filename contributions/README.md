# Contributions

Submitted `.gcp` files are collected here after review.

## How to submit

1. Use the [Master Plan GeoRef Tool](/) to create your `.gcp` file.
2. Open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/Sathvik6323/masterplan-overlay/issues/new/choose) and attach your file.
3. I'll review it, georeference it in QGIS, and deploy it.

## Georeferencing credit

Every deployed city map includes a credit: **"Georeferenced by [Your Name]"**
